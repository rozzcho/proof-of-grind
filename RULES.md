# Proof of Grind Rules

Proof of Grind is a study challenge. You pay an entry fee to join, study with your camera on in Discord, and the people who hit their goal every day split the prize pool.

These rules apply to every challenge. By registering, you agree to them.

## 1. Tracks and schedule

| | Weekly | Biweekly |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 7 days, Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59 UTC | 14 days, starting every other Monday 00:00 UTC |
| Entry fee (1x) | 7 USDC | 10 USDC |
| Daily goal | 3 hours on camera | 3 hours on camera |

- All days and times are in UTC. In Korea (UTC+9), a day runs from 09:00 to 08:59 the next morning.
- Registration for a challenge opens when the previous challenge on that track starts. It closes the moment the challenge starts.
- Biweekly is coming soon.

## 2. Entry and multiply

- Your entry is the entry fee × your multiply, from 1x to 10x.
  - Example: Weekly at 3x costs 21 USDC.
- A higher multiply means a bigger share of the prize pool if you win, and a bigger loss if you don't.
- One Discord account and one wallet per challenge.

## 3. One track at a time

- You can't be in challenges on two tracks whose dates overlap.
  - Example: while you are in a Biweekly challenge, you can't join a Weekly challenge that runs during those 14 days.
- Back-to-back challenges on the same track are fine.
  - Example: you can sign up for next week while this week's challenge is still running.
- This applies to your wallet and your Discord account alike. Switching wallets doesn't get around it.

## 4. What counts as study time

- Only time **with your camera on** in **your track's voice channel** counts.
- Screen sharing doesn't count.
- Time adds up over the day, so breaks are fine. Time on both sides of midnight UTC is counted toward each day separately.
- Your camera should show you actually studying. Your face doesn't need to fill the screen.

## 5. Passing

- Hit the daily goal on **every day** of the challenge and you win.
- Miss a single day and you're out for that challenge. There are no exceptions or make-up days.
- Your progress shows on the website under **My Challenge**.

## 6. Reports and warnings

**What gets a warning**
- Not actually studying on camera. Examples: an empty desk, a looped or pre-recorded video, a photo.
- Disrespecting other participants.

**How a report works**
1. A participant reports someone in the voice channel.
2. The bot asks **3 random participants who are in the same room** to check. The reporter doesn't vote.
3. Anyone who doesn't answer within 10 minutes is replaced by another random participant in the room.
4. If 3 answers can't be collected, the report is dropped.
5. If **at least 2 of the 3** agree, the reported person gets **one warning**.

**Consequences**
- **3 warnings in one challenge and you're out** of that challenge.
- Warnings don't carry over to your next challenge.

## 7. Fees and prize pool

- The **entry pool** is everything paid into a challenge.
- **5%** of the entry pool covers exchange fees first. Whatever is left of that 5% is the platform fee.
- The other **95%** is the **prize pool**, plus anything rolled over from an earlier challenge.

## 8. Splitting the prize

- Winners split the prize pool in proportion to their multiply.
  - Example: winners at 1x, 5x and 10x split it into 16 shares and take 1, 5 and 10 shares.
- Rewards are rounded down to 0.01 USDC. The rounding remainder goes to the platform.
- If nobody wins, the whole prize pool rolls over to the next challenge on the same track.

## 9. Server outages

- If our server goes down and your camera time can't be counted, the lost time is taken off that day's goal.
- Passed days can still be recorded until **12 hours after the challenge ends**, so a short outage doesn't cost anyone a day.
- Results are counted after that window closes, and rewards open for claiming.

## 10. Claiming your reward

- Claim your reward on the website with the **same wallet you registered with**.
- You have **4 weeks** after the challenge ends to claim.
- Rewards left unclaimed after 4 weeks go to the platform.

## 11. Staying safe

- The team will **never** ask for your wallet's secret recovery phrase or private key. Never share them.
- Sharing your wallet address is fine.
- Questions go to [#ask-the-team](https://discord.gg/scNbdXFTxq). Bugs go to [#bug-reports](https://discord.gg/wxEaVBygGk).

---

Rules can change between challenges. A challenge that has already started keeps the rules it started with.
